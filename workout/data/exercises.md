# Exercises

## Shoulder Press
id: shoulder-press
group: shoulders
kind: compound
image: /exercises/shoulder-press.webp
cue: Press from shoulders to lockout. Don't shrug the last inch.
aliases: overhead press, db press

## Lateral Raise
id: lateral-raise
group: shoulders
kind: isolation
image: /exercises/lateral-raise.webp
cue: Raise to shoulder height, slight elbow bend, control the drop.
aliases: side raise, laterals

## Bicep Curl
id: bicep-curl
group: arms
kind: isolation
image: /exercises/bicep-curl.webp
cue: Elbows pinned. Don't swing the hips into it.
aliases: curls, biceps

## Forearm Curl
id: forearm-curl
group: arms
kind: isolation
image: /exercises/forearm-curl.webp
cue: Forearms on thighs, curl with the wrists only.
aliases: forearm, wrist curl, wrists

## Bench Press
id: bench-press
group: chest
kind: compound
image: /exercises/bench-press.webp
cue: Dumbbells over mid-chest, slight elbow tuck. For when the bench shows up.
aliases: db bench, chest press

## Dumbbell Row
id: dumbbell-row
group: back
kind: compound
image: /exercises/dumbbell-row.webp
cue: Flat back, pull the bell to your hip, squeeze the shoulder blade.
aliases: row, one arm row

## Goblet Squat
id: goblet-squat
group: legs
kind: compound
image: /exercises/goblet-squat.webp
cue: Bell at your chest, elbows inside knees, sit between the heels.
aliases: squat

## Romanian Deadlift
id: rdl
group: legs
kind: compound
image: /exercises/rdl.webp
cue: Soft knees, push the hips back, bells stay close to your legs.
aliases: romanian deadlift, rdls

## Tricep Extension
id: tricep-extension
group: arms
kind: isolation
image: /exercises/tricep-extension.webp
cue: Elbows up, lower behind the head, don't flare.
aliases: overhead extension, triceps

## Hammer Curl
id: hammer-curl
group: arms
kind: isolation
image: /exercises/hammer-curl.webp
cue: Palms face each other the whole way. Same rule as curls: no swinging.
aliases: hammers
